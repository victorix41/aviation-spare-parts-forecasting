# Governance and Controls

## 1. Purpose

The Aviation Spare Parts Demand Forecasting platform is a management
decision-support system.

It does not have authority to execute operational, procurement,
engineering, financial or airworthiness decisions.

## 2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Human approval is mandatory.

Recommendations generated by analytics, forecasting, inventory
optimisation or agentic AI must be reviewed by appropriately authorised
personnel.

## 3. Procurement Controls

The platform must not automatically:

- create purchase orders;
- approve purchase orders;
- commit expenditure;
- select suppliers on behalf of authorised personnel.

Procurement recommendations are advisory.

## 4. Inventory Controls

The platform must not automatically:

- change inventory balances;
- issue inventory;
- receive inventory;
- amend stock records.

Inventory optimisation outputs are recommendations only.

## 5. Financial Controls

The platform must not:

- approve expenditure;
- authorise budgets;
- execute payments;
- replace Finance Manager review.

## 6. Engineering Controls

Engineering-related recommendations do not constitute engineering
approval or airworthiness approval.

Engineering decisions remain subject to authorised engineering review.

## 7. Quality Controls

Quality recommendations and assurance findings are decision-support
information.

They do not replace required quality-system review, approval or
regulatory compliance activities.

## 8. Forecast Governance

Forecasts are estimates based on available historical data.

Model selection, forecast confidence and underlying data quality should
be considered before management action is taken.

## 9. Agentic AI Governance

The agentic component operates in deterministic advisory mode.

Controls include:

- no database writes by agents;
- no inventory changes;
- no purchase-order creation;
- no financial approval;
- human approval required;
- evidence required;
- final assurance required.

## 10. Management Decision Governance

The platform provides a controlled mechanism for recording management
review of assured recommendations.

Permitted management decision states are:

- Accepted
- Deferred
- Rejected

A management decision must include a decision reason.

An Accepted decision means that the recommendation has been accepted for
management decision-support purposes only.

It does not constitute:

- purchase-order approval;
- expenditure approval;
- inventory authorisation;
- engineering approval;
- airworthiness approval;
- quality approval; or
- operational execution.

Recording a management decision creates an audit record only.

Human management authority remains outside the automated analytical and
agentic components of the platform.

## 11. Management Decision Audit Trail

Management decisions are retained in a dedicated management audit
database.

The audit trail supports traceability of information including:

- audit identifier;
- recording timestamp;
- recommendation identifier;
- spare-part number;
- advisory agent;
- target management role;
- recommendation type;
- recommendation priority;
- assurance status;
- forecast confidence;
- management decision;
- decision reason;
- reviewer reference;
- human-approval requirement;
- automatic-action permission; and
- source recommendation evidence.

Management decision records are retained for governance, assurance and
management review.

The audit database does not provide authority to modify operational
systems.

## 12. Decision Analytics Governance

Management decision analytics are derived from recorded audit data.

The analytics may be used to review decision volumes, decision outcomes,
management roles, recommendation priorities and recent decision
activity.

Decision analytics are descriptive management information only.

They do not constitute approval authority and must not trigger automatic
procurement, inventory, financial, engineering, quality or operational
actions.

## 13. Concurrent-Execution Control

Scheduled execution uses an exclusive lock.

Only the process that successfully acquires a lock may release that
lock.

This prevents one blocked process from deleting another process's
execution lock.

## 14. Auditability

The platform retains auditable information including:

- pipeline execution results;
- pipeline-stage results;
- scheduled-job summaries;
- scheduled-job logs;
- agentic advisory outputs;
- assurance findings;
- management-report metadata;
- production-readiness results;
- management decision audit records;
- management decision analytics;
- data-quality monitoring results;
- management alerts and exceptions;
- governance-assurance results,

## 15. Production Readiness

Production readiness is validated using:

```bash
python -m src.validation.run_production_readiness
```

A release should not proceed unless the result is:

```text
PRODUCTION READINESS: PASSED
```
