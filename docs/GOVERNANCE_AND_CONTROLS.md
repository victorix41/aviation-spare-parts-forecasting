# Governance and Controls

## 1. Purpose

The Aviation Spare Parts Demand Forecasting platform is a management
decision-support system.

It does not have authority to execute operational, procurement,
engineering, financial or airworthiness decisions.

## 2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Human approval is mandatory.

Recommendations generated by analytics, forecasting, inventory
optimisation or agentic AI must be reviewed by appropriately authorised
personnel.

## 3. Procurement Controls

The platform must not automatically:

- create purchase orders;
- approve purchase orders;
- commit expenditure;
- select suppliers on behalf of authorised personnel.

Procurement recommendations are advisory.

## 4. Inventory Controls

The platform must not automatically:

- change inventory balances;
- issue inventory;
- receive inventory;
- amend stock records.

Inventory optimisation outputs are recommendations only.

## 5. Financial Controls

The platform must not:

- approve expenditure;
- authorise budgets;
- execute payments;
- replace Finance Manager review.

## 6. Engineering Controls

Engineering-related recommendations do not constitute engineering
approval or airworthiness approval.

Engineering decisions remain subject to authorised engineering review.

## 7. Quality Controls

Quality recommendations and assurance findings are decision-support
information.

They do not replace required quality-system review, approval or
regulatory compliance activities.

## 8. Forecast Governance

Forecasts are estimates based on available historical data.

Model selection, forecast confidence and underlying data quality should
be considered before management action is taken.

## 9. Agentic AI Governance

The agentic component operates in deterministic advisory mode.

Controls include:

- no database writes by agents;
- no inventory changes;
- no purchase-order creation;
- no financial approval;
- human approval required;
- evidence required;
- final assurance required.

## 10. Concurrent-Execution Control

Scheduled execution uses an exclusive lock.

Only the process that successfully acquires a lock may release that
lock.

This prevents one blocked process from deleting another process's
execution lock.

## 11. Auditability

The platform retains auditable information including:

- pipeline execution results;
- pipeline-stage results;
- scheduled-job summaries;
- scheduled-job logs;
- agentic advisory outputs;
- assurance findings;
- management-report metadata;
- production-readiness results.

## 12. Production Readiness

Production readiness is validated using:

```bash
python -m src.validation.run_production_readiness
```

A release should not proceed unless the result is:

```text
PRODUCTION READINESS: PASSED
```
